How to install
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the button above.
  2. On the Expo page, tap Install and download the APK.
  3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
  4. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. Play Protect may show a warning, since the app is not from the Play Store.
